# Part003, Cue, reward and motor

This was developed as a part of Airtrack, [URL] of main document.
Look there for more information on the context where this part was designed, as well as get information on the developers and the community.


### Hardware overview

To fulfill all needed tasks, the Airtrack needs a cue, rewards and motor to move the reward to the right spot.
As a cue, two LEDs are placed left and right to the front of the mouse at some rods to elevate them above the moving platform.

The reward liquid is placed somewhere besides the air table and a tube goes to the front of the motor.
The motor is located centrally above the air table parallel to the long axis.
It is a linear actuator which moves the licking spot down and towards the mouse.
