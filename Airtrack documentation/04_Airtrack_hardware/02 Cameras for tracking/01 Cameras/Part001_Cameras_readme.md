In the setup are eight cameras placed to acquire videos of various body parts of the mouse. Two cameras track the eyes, another two the whiskers, one for the front head view, one for the back and tail placement and two top view cameras (one wide view with platform and one for the hole mouse from the top). The cameras are mounted at the Thorlabs plates around and above the air table.

All cameras have the same configuration which is then placed in the setup. Each camera has a Trigger cables for the I/O connection, a Basler USB cable and combination of a Fisso arm with Thorlabs rods to place the camera.

The Basler USB cable and I/O cable is often very long and needs good pathing to the acquiring computer and frame trigger source.

Basler USB card most likely needed for bandwidth on PC
