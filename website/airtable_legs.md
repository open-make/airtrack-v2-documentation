# Air table part 7: Mounting of air table legs

We chose to use legs with adjustable height to ensure a perfect even air table. If the air table is not even, the platform drifts to one side and by this induces a bias of the mouse to one side. Additionally to that one can counter biases of mice by making the platform uneven. Some mice tend to prefer e.g. the left lane.

##  Air table legs

- First add 2 [nuts](connectors.yml#4mmNuts){Qty:4} to each [aluminium strut profiles (length: 480mm)](fromstep)
    - The nuts need to point outwards

- Place in each [nut](connectors.yml#4mmNuts) an [optical post (length: 50mm)](thorlabs_parts.yml#tr50m){Qty:4}. 
    - The [optical post](thorlabs_parts.yml#tr50m) is delivered with a M4 threaded rod, use that.

- To each [optical post](thorlabs_parts.yml#tr50m) add a [90 degree angle](thorlabs_parts.yml#ra50m){Qty:4}.

- To achieve the verticality, put in each [90 degree angle](thorlabs_parts.yml#ra50m) an  [optical post (length: 100mm)](thorlabs_parts.yml#tr100m){Qty: 4}.

- Finally align the vertical optical posts with the threads of your breadboard or table and fixate them using M6 threaded rods with a length of 20mm. These threaded rods are part of the [M6 Cap Screw and Hardware Kit ](thorlabs_parts.yml#hwkit2m).
