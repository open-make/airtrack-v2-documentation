# Airtable part 1: Precut of the aluminium profile strut


This section provides information to precut the [aluminium strut profile](framework.yml#20x20Rod){Qty: 2} and labels the different parts based on length.

For cutting the struts a [metall saw](tools.yml#metallsaw){Qty: 1, Cat:tool} is needed (see link for saw recommendation). 
{{BOM}}



### Pieces out of  2000mm aluminium strut profile #1

- Cut three pieces with a length of 450 mm out of one aluminium strut profile. 

- You have now three [aluminium strut profiles (length: 450mm)]{output, Qty: 3}.



### Pieces out of  2000mm aluminium strut profile #2

- Cut two pieces with a length of 480 mm out of one 2000 mm aluminium strut profile.

- Cut one pieces with a length of 150 mm out of the remaining 2000 mm aluminium strut profile.

- You have now two [aluminium strut profiles (length: 480mm)]{output, Qty: 2} and one  [aluminium strut profiles (length: 150mm)]{output, Qty: 1}.
